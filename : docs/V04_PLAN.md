# Reality Duel — V04 Plan

## Vision
Reality Duel is a platform for discovering, showcasing, and connecting real talent through challenges and opportunities.

## Core Features

### 1. Talent Profile
- Personal talent profile
- Skills and abilities
- Portfolio and achievements
- Challenge history

### 2. Discover Talent
- Browse talented users
- Search by skills
- View talent profiles
- Discover emerging talent

### 3. Opportunities
- Job and project opportunities
- Talent matching
- Company and organization opportunities

### 4. Challenges
- Talent challenges
- Competitions
- Results and rankings
- Showcase real abilities

## Business Model
The platform should remain accessible to users without charging users for participation.

Revenue can come from:
- Companies and organizations
- Talent discovery and recruitment tools
- Success fees from hiring
- Sponsored challenges and tournaments
- Universities and institutions
- Reports and certifications

## V04 Goal
Build a clear MVP foundation that can evolve into a scalable talent discovery platform.
